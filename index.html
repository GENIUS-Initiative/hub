About

What the GENIUS Initiative is building

A CDE-funded, multi-county educational equity program grounded in the STAAL framework — giving schools shared language, shared tools, and a shared community for the work of protecting and nurturing every student's inherent genius.

The GENIUS Initiative connects school teams across Los Angeles, Kings, and Sonoma counties, pairing principal coaching with Communities of Practice so that leaders aren't doing this work alone. Everything on this hub — the convenings, the coaching tools, the resource library — ties back to the four principles of the STAAL Guide.

1
Explore Belief Systems to Begin the Work
Naming and examining the beliefs that shape how students are seen.
2
Create Educational Systems that Facilitate and Support Learning
Building structures that make strong instruction sustainable.
3
Ensure Culturally Relevant, Sustaining, and Revitalizing High Quality Instruction
Instruction that reflects and affirms students' identities.
4
Mitigate the Accumulation of Disadvantage
Catching and interrupting patterns before they compound.
